# Agent 2 Romanian Outreach Rules

## Purpose

Romanian outreach requires fundamentally different communication patterns than English/American cold outreach. This specification defines native Romanian business communication behaviors that build trust and generate responses.

### Cultural Context
- **Trust sensitivity** - Romanian business culture is more skeptical of unsolicited outreach
- **Directness preference** - Romanian professionals value concise, direct communication
- **Anti-hype mentality** - Exaggerated claims and enthusiasm trigger immediate distrust
- **Language authenticity** - Emails must feel naturally Romanian, not translated English

## Formality Rules

### Respectful B2B Romanian
- Use "dumneavoastră / vă" for professional respect
- Maintain appropriate business distance
- Avoid overly casual language
- Balance formality with natural flow

### Preferred Patterns
**GOOD Romanian:**
- "Am observat..." - Natural observation opening
- "Vă trimit..." - Direct and helpful
- "Are sens să..." - Logical reasoning
- "Am văzut că..." - Simple, factual
- "Dacă vreți..." - Respectful suggestion

**AVOID Romanian:**
- "Stimate domn/doamnă" - Overly formal, dated
- "Cu stimă" - Old-fashioned business language
- "Vă contactez pentru a vă prezenta" - Corporate jargon
- "În speranța unei colaborări" - Weak, overly polite

## Tone Rules

### Voice Characteristics
- **Concise** - Romanian business communication values brevity
- **Calm confidence** - Understated authority, not hype
- **Observational** - Focus on concrete observations
- **Commercially aware** - Business-savvy but practical
- **Natural Romanian** - Native phrasing, not translated patterns

### Forbidden Tonal Elements
- **Fake enthusiasm** - Artificial excitement sounds suspicious
- **Aggressive sales** - Pushy language triggers rejection
- **Corporate jargon** - Business buzzwords create distance
- **AI structure** - Formulaic patterns sound automated
- **Over-friendliness** - False familiarity breaks trust

## Romanian Business Psychology

### Distrust Triggers
**Immediate Rejection Factors:**
- **Obvious agency jargon** - "soluții digitale", "strategie digitală"
- **Fake enthusiasm** - Exclamation points, overly positive language
- **Exaggerated promises** - "rezultate garantate", "creștere exponențială"
- **Long consultant explanations** - Romanian business prefers directness
- **Translated English patterns** - "Vă pot ajuta să creșteți vizibilitatea"
- **Sales-guru language** - "transformare digitală", "partener strategic"
- **AI-sounding structure** - Formulaic introductions and conclusions
- **Generic personalization** - "Am văzut site-ul dumneavoastră impresionant"

### Trust Builders
- **Specific observations** - Concrete details from their website
- **Understated confidence** - Calm, knowledgeable tone
- **Natural Romanian flow** - Native phrasing patterns
- **Respectful distance** - Professional but not cold
- **Logical reasoning** - "Are sens să..." approach

## CTA Rules

### Low-Friction Romanian Philosophy
First email CTAs must feel easy to answer without creating pressure or commitment.

### Good Romanian CTA Examples
- "Merită să vă trimit observațiile?" - Curious, respectful
- "Vreți să vedeți ce am observat?" - Direct, intriguing
- "Are sens să vă trimit varianta scurtă?" - Logical, helpful
- "Vă trimit cele 2-3 idei?" - Specific, low commitment
- "Dacă e util, vă trimit detaliile" - Conditional, no pressure

### Bad Romanian CTA Examples
- "Ați fi deschiși la o discuție?" - High friction, formal
- "Programați un call" - English loanword, aggressive
- "Când aveți timp pentru o întâlnire?" - Assumes interest, creates work
- "Putem discuta cum vă putem ajuta?" - Vague, sales-oriented

### CTA Principles
- **Mobile-friendly** - Easy to read and respond quickly
- **One-click mental effort** - Simple yes/no type questions
- **Specific value** - Clear what they'll receive
- **No scheduling** - Save meeting requests for follow-up emails

## Romanian Banned Phrases

### Corporate Jargon (Immediate Trust Killers)
- **"prezență online"** - Generic agency speak
- **"soluții digitale"** - Vague, meaningless
- **"strategie digitală"** - Corporate jargon
- **"optimizare"** - Overused consultant term
- **"creșterea vizibilității"** - Empty marketing promise
- **"transformare digitală"** - Hype phrase
- **"vă putem ajuta"** - Weak, sales-oriented
- **"o discuție scurtă"** - Understates commitment
- **"partener strategic"** - Presumptuous, corporate

### Why These Fail in Romanian Context
- **Sound translated** - Not native business communication
- **Create distance** - Agency language feels impersonal
- **Trigger skepticism** - Romanian business culture distrusts hype
- **Lack specificity** - Vague promises sound suspicious

## Subject Line Rules

### Romanian Subject Philosophy
Subjects should create curiosity while maintaining professional credibility and native Romanian flow.

### Good Romanian Subject Examples
- "O observație despre Visiodent" - Specific, intriguing
- "Prețul de pe site" - Direct, relevant observation
- "Un lucru observat" - Simple, curiosity-driven
- "Site-ul dumneavoastră" - Personalized, direct
- "O întrebare despre..." - Curious, respectful

### Bad Romanian Subject Examples
- "Oportunitate de colaborare" - Generic, agency-sounding
- "Servicii marketing digital" - Corporate jargon
- "Creșterea vizibilității online" - Hype, spammy
- "Parteneriat strategic" - Presumptuous, formal
- "Soluții pentru afacerea dumneavoastră" - Vague, corporate

### Subject Principles
- **Short and specific** - Under 50 characters when possible
- **Observation-based** - Reference concrete details
- **Curiosity-driven** - Make them want to learn more
- **Native Romanian** - Avoid English loanwords and corporate phrases
- **Anti-spam** - Avoid trigger words and hype

## Good Romanian Email Example

**Subject:** O observație despre site

**Body:**
Bună ziua,

Am observat că pe site-ul dumneavoastră nu sunt afișate prețurile pentru serviciile de imaging. Multe clinici din România evită să contacteze furnizori fără informații de preț clare.

Am văzut că specializați pe echipamente dentare digitale. Are sens să vă trimit câteva observații despre cum alte clinici prezintă transparent prețurile?

Dacă e util, vă trimit detaliile.

**Why This Works:**
- Natural Romanian flow
- Specific observation (missing prices)
- Logical reasoning ("Are sens să...")
- Low-friction CTA
- Respectful but not overly formal
- No corporate jargon

## Bad Romanian Email Example

**Subject:** Oportunitate de colaborare strategică

**Body:**
Stimate domnule,

Vă contactez în speranța unei colaborări strategice. Compania noastră oferă soluții digitale inovatoare pentru creșterea vizibilității online. Am analizat site-ul dumneavoastră și considerăm că putem optimiza prezența digitală pentru a maximiza potențialul de business.

Ați fi deschiși la o discuție scurtă despre cum vă putem ajuta să transformați digital afacerea?

**Why This Fails:**
- Overly formal and dated language
- Corporate jargon ("soluții digitale", "prezență digitală")
- Hype phrases ("creșterea vizibilității", "transformați digital")
- Translated English patterns
- High-friction CTA
- Fake enthusiasm
- Sounds like generic agency outreach

## Multi-Friction Website Teardown Rule

Agent 2 should usually avoid building the email around only one isolated problem unless the packet clearly contains only one usable signal.

Preferred framing:
- mention that the site has several small friction points together
- use 2-3 concrete observations from the packet
- connect them to hesitation, trust, clarity, or contact friction
- do not turn the email into a long audit
- do not list too many problems aggressively

Good framing:
"Am observat câteva lucruri care, împreună, pot face site-ul mai puțin convingător decât ar putea fi: mesajul principal nu e foarte clar din prima, unele informații importante sunt greu de găsit, iar partea de încredere ar putea fi susținută mai bine."

Bad framing:
"M-am uitat pe site și lipsește prețul."

Important:
Keep the tone sharp, direct, and practical.
Avoid consultant language.
Avoid sounding like a translated marketing agency.

The Romanian outreach specification demonstrates that effective multilingual communication requires cultural behavior adaptation, not just language translation.
